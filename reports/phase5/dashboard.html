Anomaly & Outlier Detection

Records flagged as anomalous by at least 2 of 3 independent methods (IQR, Z-score, Isolation Forest) shown in the same PCA space as the segment map above. 71.5% of the strictest (all-3-methods) anomalies turned out to be a missing-data imputation artifact, not a genuine financial extreme — see the callout.

Data-quality finding: income/property_value missing values were imputed with the raw dataset mean in Phase 1. Because the inverse-transform is lossless, any record carrying that exact placeholder value is detectable. 71.5% of records flagged by all 3 anomaly-detection methods together turned out to be this imputation artifact rather than a genuine financial extreme — a reminder to check why a record is flagged before escalating it.
